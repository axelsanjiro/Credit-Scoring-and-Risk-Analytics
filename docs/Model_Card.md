# Model Card

## Intended Use

Estimate Probability of Default for historical Lending Club applications. Use for education and portfolio analysis only. Do not use as production lending policy.

## Data And Features

Training data covers June 2007 through November 2014. Validation covers December 2014 through August 2016. Final test covers September 2016 through December 2018.

Model uses 49 approved pre-origination predictors, including affordability, revolving-credit, account-history, and deterministic financial-ratio features. `issue_date` is split-only. Conditional pricing fields `int_rate` and `installment` are excluded.

`open_rv_12m` and `open_rv_24m` are excluded because training period has no observed values.

## Model Selection

The experiment benchmarks Logistic Regression, LightGBM, XGBoost, and CatBoost on the same chronological validation period. The selected model and validation metrics are recorded in the generated `models/final_model_report.json`.

Optuna evaluates four LightGBM trials using three expanding temporal folds. Tuned LightGBM does not beat XGBoost validation ROC-AUC.

Sigmoid and isotonic calibration are fitted on the earlier part of the validation period and compared on its later part. The selected method is then refit on the complete validation period before final test evaluation.

## Final Test Results

Final test metrics are generated by `python -m src.experiment` and saved to `models/final_model_report.json`. They should be regenerated whenever the data, feature contract, calibration procedure, or model configuration changes.

The experiment also writes risk-decile, cutoff, feature-importance, and SHAP reports to `models/` for review.

Global SHAP review ranks `term_months`, `acc_open_past_24mths`, and `loan_to_income` as top drivers. `term_months` requires continued decision-point and policy-dependence review.

## Decision Policy

Expected Loss = PD x LGD x EAD. Assumptions: LGD `45%`, annual margin `7%`, operational cost `$100` per approved loan, maximum approved default rate `15%`.

The recommended cutoff, approval rate, observed approved default rate, and expected profit are generated from the final test predictions under these assumptions.

## Limitations And Monitoring

Compare validation and final-test performance after every experiment run to detect temporal drift across vintages.

Lending Club outcomes are historical, platform-specific, and not representative of every lending population. Geographic proxies and protected attributes are excluded. Fairness analysis needs legally permitted demographic data and policy review before real use.

Monitor feature missingness, population stability, approval rate, default rate, calibration by decile, discrimination, and profit assumptions each new vintage.
